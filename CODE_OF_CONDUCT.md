# Code of Conduct

MARGINAL is built in public for researchers, developers, operators, and organizations with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and criticize ideas rather than people;
- welcome clear questions and good-faith disagreement;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized conduct;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- represent benchmarks, security findings, and project capabilities honestly.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when behavior harms
the community or project. Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed confidentially and without retaliation.
